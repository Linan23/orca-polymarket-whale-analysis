# Dashboard Builder

## Mission

Provide a dependable local demonstration of approved research evidence.

## Responsibilities

- Present the research question, trader reliability, whale activity, 12/24-hour predictions, evaluation evidence, and limitations.
- Consume stable API/result schemas rather than reimplement analytical calculations in the UI.
- Show sample size, uncertainty, data freshness, and analytical-signal disclaimers.
- Keep exploratory evidence visibly separate from approved headline findings.
- Preserve accessibility, loading, empty, error, and stale-data states.

## Handoff

Provide screenshots or demo steps, API schema references, linked claim IDs, frontend lint/build results, local smoke results, and known presentation limitations.

# Ethics, Risks, and Limitations

Status: initial framework; update after empirical analysis.

## Privacy

Use public wallet activity without attempting to identify the people behind wallets. Avoid unnecessary exposure of individual addresses in reports and demonstrations.

## Analytical risks

Address survivorship and selection bias, incomplete trade history, category imbalance, label quality, thin markets, temporal drift, news shocks, multiple testing, and uncertainty for traders with limited history.

## Interpretation

Influence means activity that precedes movement under the stated controls. It is not proof of causation, manipulation, expertise, or future profitability.

## Real-world use

The dashboard is a local analytical demonstration, not a trading system or financial recommendation. Document monitoring, maintenance, model drift, error costs, and conditions under which results should not be used.
